Ripe

White and Orange Edition

The Orange and Amber colours of wines are born from time: white grape varieties left to macerate with their skins, held in that threshold between white to orange. For this edition, we meditate on how time affect colour and flavour, what remains and what does not.

We are taking you on a liquid and sonic journey: minerality will guide us through geography: from the Mediterranean coast, Corsica, Puglia, then going north to Friuli-Venezia Giulia, before a leap to Georgia. Four regions, one thread. Minerality as a journey.

Ripe is back for a second edition. After our first gathering around red wines and the rhythms of farming seasons, we return with a tasting of a whole hue of white, amber and orange wines, paired with sound and performance. This time, Maryisonacid and Pierre Renard will guide you through four distinct wine regions, moving through their terroir and musicality.

The evening unfolds in conversation. We'll taste four wines together, share the basics of wine tasting as we go: how to read a wine, how to decipher its profile, what to listen for in a glass. For each glass, some local melody selected by Maryisonacid. The programme culminates in a live performance of Georgian folkloric dance.
