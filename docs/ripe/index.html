Ripe

White and Orange Edition

Flyer

Orange wine is born from time: white grape varieties left to macerate with their skins, held in that threshold between white to orange. For this edition, we meditate on which colour remains and which ones do not.

We are taking you on a liquid and sonic journey: minerality will guide us through geography: from the Mediterranean coast, Corsica, Puglia, before a leap to Georgia. Three regions, one thread. Minerality as a journey.

Ripe is back for a second edition. After our first gathering around red wines and the rhythms of farming seasons, we return with a tasting of white and orange wines paired with sound and performance. This time, Maryisonacid and Pierre Renard will guide you through three distinct wine regions, moving through their profiles and the rhythm they hold.

The evening unfolds in conversation. We'll taste three to four wines together, share the basics of wine tasting as we go: how to read a wine, how to decipher its profile, what to listen for in a glass. Then we'll turn to the sound, to what Maryisonacid has created in response to these wines and this geography. The programme culminates in a live performance of Georgian folkloric dance to close the night.
