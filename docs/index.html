Get in touch

I support artists and arts organisations to develop their visions within space, time, and budget, across live performance, live music, the club scene, and contemporary art.

Based in Berlin for over a decade, I have built a practice spanning both institutional and independent contexts, producing large-scale live events, developing dance programmes, and serving on national jury panels for choreography. My work moves fluidly between production and curation, bringing the same rigour to a 50-person event as to a 10,000+ person festival. Over the years I have built a reliable network of collaborators across every discipline, from sound to lighting design, front of house team, artists, and music producers. I am able to source the right set of partners from alternative to institutional contexts.

Curatorial Projects

My curatorial practice moves across contemporary dance, ballet, urban and performative forms, with a consistent interest in site-specific work that plays on the porosity between audience and performance. I am working toward a deconstruction of traditional theatre times, the beginning and the end are structural markers of many venues, my work tends to fade them away, turning cultural spaces into a catalyst for bonding and lingering together. The work gathered below is made exclusively with artists who operate at the edges of their disciplines, often shaped by queer perspectives and marginalised voices.

My experience ranges from artist residencies to fully realised performances, and large-scale live events. For several years I shaped the dance programme at TRAUMA Arts and Culture in Berlin, consistently oriented toward experimentation and early-stage artists, providing infrastructure and curatorial support for work for emerging local artists and also for international artists. More recently, I have been developing Ripe, an independent series pairing wine with live performance and music. The next event is coming up on September 24th.

Workshops

A series of long-format workshops designed for professional movers and dedicated amateurs, co-conceptualised with the instructors and hosted at TRAUMA's dance studio. Each workshop approached a single theme from multiple perspectives, bridging theory and practice within an intimate group setting.

  • Body of the Heart: Tools for Openness

    Michelle Moura & Elisabete Finger

    A workshop exploring the psychophysical dimensions that move us, drawing on tools from Kundalini Yoga and contemporary dance practice.

  • Holding Each Other: Moving and Writing Through Vulnerability

    Sorour Darabi, Saura & oxi pëng

    A five-hour journey through collective vulnerability, weaving movement, writing, and shared sensory experience into bubbly manifestos and bodily archives.

  • Caretaking: Tools for Sustaining in the Berlin Dance Scene

    Cree Barnett Williams & Shannon Leypoldt

    A workshop and discussion for freelance dancers in Berlin, exchanging resources, care strategies, and tools for sustaining long-term dance careers.

  • Death in Spring

    Saura

    A yoga session touching on death and life as a full cycle, exploring each pose as if for the first and last time.

  • Vibrations

    Saura

    An active meditation journey exploring physical and introspective vibrations, set within TRAUMA's club floor transformed into a yoga space.

  • Holiday Grounding Session

    Saura

    An extended yoga class drawing from the Ashtanga Primary series, releasing the energies of the year through breathwork, flexibility, and shared tea.
